    Import into Grok Bot
    

    Open Grok Bot ↗
    

    1. Open the Grok Bot app and choose New Bot (the + button, or Ctrl/⌘+N).
    2. Name it your bot and choose Create.
    3. Paste the setup as your first message. The bot saves the rules.
    4. To share it, open the bot's details and choose Share, then Create a copy, then Publish.
    

    "Open Grok Bot" goes to the official Grok Bot page (x.ai/bot), where you can download the app. There is no public link that creates a bot for you, so you paste the setup yourself.
